# GCSFT Lesson Plans

The shared vocabulary used across GCSFT lesson plans and kneeboard cards, so every instructor teaches and briefs to the same meaning.

## Stalling

**Straight and steady stall**:
A stall entered wings level, at about 1 g and in balance, with no deliberate yaw — e.g. power-off or 50% power clean stalls, the approach-configuration stall, and impending stalls in slow flight.
_Avoid_: Basic stall, standard stall

**Stall recovery altitude**:
The height (AGL) by which a stall recovery must be complete: 2500 ft for a straight and steady stall, 3000 ft for every other stall (turning, slipping, climbing, scenario, accelerated).
_Avoid_: Stall height, minimum stall altitude

**Pro-spin input**:
A yaw or aileron input that drives the aircraft toward a spin — rudder toward a dropping wing or into a skid, or aileron to raise a dropped wing. Not permitted below Va, by student or instructor, not even to induce a wing drop.
_Avoid_: Spin input, crossed controls

**Pro-recovery input**:
A control input that tends to return a stalled aircraft to wings level — e.g. the opposite rudder of a slipping turn, whose stall drops the outer wing toward level.
_Avoid_: Anti-spin input
